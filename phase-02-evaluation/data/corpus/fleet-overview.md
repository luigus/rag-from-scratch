# Aurora Fleet Overview

*Fictional operator: Atlantic Rail. All data in this corpus is invented for testing.*

## The trains

The Aurora class 450 is an electric multiple unit (EMU) operated by Atlantic Rail on the coastal main line between Lisbon and Faro. The fleet has 24 units, delivered between 2021 and 2023.

Each unit has 6 cars and is 152 metres long. A unit offers 412 seats, of which 40 are first class. Two units can run coupled together, giving 824 seats at peak hours.

The maximum operating speed in passenger service is 200 km/h. The design speed is 220 km/h, which is only used during acceptance tests.

## Power and traction

The trains draw power from the overhead line at 25 kV 50 Hz AC through a single pantograph on car 3. Total traction power is 4.8 MW, distributed over 12 powered axles.

Regenerative braking returns energy to the overhead line when the train slows down. On the Lisbon–Faro route, regenerative braking recovers up to 18% of the traction energy.

## Train protection

The fleet is fitted with ETCS Level 2 for the main line and with the legacy Convel system for branch lines that are not yet upgraded.

## Depots

Heavy maintenance is done at Oriente Depot in Lisbon, which has the wheel lathe and the bogie workshop. Faro Depot only handles daily inspections, cleaning and light repairs.
