# Signalling and Safety Rules

*Fictional operator: Atlantic Rail. All data in this corpus is invented for testing.*

## Train protection systems

The main line between Lisbon and Faro is equipped with ETCS Level 2. Movement authorities are sent to the train by radio, and there are no lineside signals on this section. Branch lines still use the Convel system with lineside signals.

## Driver vigilance

The driver must acknowledge the vigilance device every 60 seconds. If there is no acknowledgement, an audible warning sounds, and the emergency brake is applied automatically after 3 seconds.

## Speed limits

The line speed is 200 km/h on most of the main line. Inside the Serra Tunnel the speed limit is 120 km/h, because of the tunnel's aerodynamic effects. When ETCS is in degraded mode and the train runs under Staff Responsible mode, the maximum speed is 40 km/h.

## Braking distances

From 200 km/h, the emergency braking distance of an Aurora unit is about 1,850 metres on level track. Service braking from the same speed needs about 2,600 metres.

## Fire on board

If a fire is detected on board, the train must continue to the next station or to a safe place outside tunnels, where passengers can be evacuated. The train only stops immediately if the fire affects the traction system and continuing is no longer possible.
