# Aurora Maintenance Manual

*Fictional operator: Atlantic Rail. All data in this corpus is invented for testing.*

## Inspection plan

Maintenance follows a plan of inspections of increasing depth:

- **A-check**: a visual inspection done every 24 hours of service, at either depot.
- **B-check**: done every 15,000 km. Includes brakes, doors, couplers and the pantograph.
- **C-check**: done every 120,000 km. Includes a full test of the traction and braking systems.
- **Bogie overhaul**: every 1.2 million km or 8 years, whichever comes first. Only done at Oriente Depot.

## Brakes

The brake pads have the part number BRK-4471. They must be replaced when the pad thickness is below 7 mm. After replacing the pads, a static brake test is mandatory before the unit returns to service.

## Wheels

Wheels are reprofiled on the wheel lathe when the flange thickness is below 26 mm. A wheel that can no longer be reprofiled within limits must be replaced; the minimum wheel diameter is 790 mm.

## Pantograph

The pantograph carbon strips have the part number PNT-0932. They are replaced when only 10 mm of carbon remains. A broken carbon strip must be reported immediately to the infrastructure manager, because it can damage the overhead line.

## Air conditioning

The HVAC filters are changed every 30 days during the summer season (June to September) and every 60 days during the rest of the year.

## Fault codes

The train management system shows fault codes on the driver's display. The most frequent are:

- **F-217**: door obstacle detection fault. The door is locked out of service and the train can continue.
- **F-305**: pantograph low air pressure. The driver must lower the pantograph and contact the control centre.
- **F-412**: HVAC compressor overload. The air conditioning of the affected car switches to ventilation only.
