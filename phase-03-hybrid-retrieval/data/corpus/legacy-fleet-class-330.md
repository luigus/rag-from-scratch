# Class 330 Regional Fleet

*Fictional operator: Atlantic Rail. All data in this corpus is invented for testing.*

## The trains

The Class 330 is the older regional fleet of Atlantic Rail, used on the branch lines. The Class 330 has a maximum speed of 160 km/h. Each unit has 4 cars and 248 seats, all in second class. The fleet is maintained at Entroncamento Depot.

The Class 330 only has the Convel train protection system, so it cannot run on the ETCS-only sections of the main line.

## Maintenance intervals

The Class 330 follows a lighter maintenance plan than the newer trains:

- The A-check is done every 48 hours of service.
- The B-check interval is 10,000 km.
- The C-check interval is 90,000 km.
- Bogies are overhauled every 900,000 km or 6 years, whichever comes first.

## Brakes and pantograph

Brake pads BRK-4417 are replaced once they are thinner than 9 mm. The pantograph carbon strips of the Class 330 are replaced when 12 mm of carbon is left, because the older pantograph presses harder on the overhead line.
