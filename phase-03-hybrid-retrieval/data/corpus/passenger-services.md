# Passenger Services on Aurora Trains

*Fictional operator: Atlantic Rail. All data in this corpus is invented for testing.*

## Bicycles

Each train carries up to 8 bicycles, in the dedicated areas of cars 1 and 6. A bicycle reservation is mandatory on weekends and public holidays. On weekdays, bicycles are accepted without a reservation while space is available.

## Accessibility

There are 4 wheelchair spaces per unit, all in car 3, next to the accessible toilet. Passengers who need help boarding should request assistance at least 24 hours before travelling.

## Pets

Small pets travelling in a carrier travel free of charge. Large dogs must wear a muzzle and a leash, and they pay half of a second class fare.

## Quiet car

Car 5 is the quiet car. Phone calls and loud conversations are not allowed there.

## On-board services

Free Wi-Fi is available in all cars. A catering trolley serves drinks and snacks on journeys longer than 2 hours.

## Delay compensation

Passengers are compensated when their train arrives late at the final destination:

- a delay of 60 to 119 minutes gives a refund of 25% of the ticket price;
- a delay of 120 minutes or more gives a refund of 50% of the ticket price.

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the journey.
